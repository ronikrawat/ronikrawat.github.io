🌸 4-Week Fertility Wellness Plan

Hypothyroidism • Hypertension • Conception Support

📅 Monthly Goals

  • Protein: 70–80g daily
  • Water: 2–2.5 Liters daily
  • 2 Fruits + 4–5 Vegetable servings
  • Fish: 2–3 times weekly
  • 1–2 Eggs daily
  • 1 tbsp Flax Seeds daily
  • 1 tbsp Pumpkin Seeds daily
  • 150–210 minutes walking weekly
  • 7.5–8.5 hours sleep

🗓 Weekly Plan

Week 1 - Foundation

Focus: Build healthy habits and consistency.

Activity: Walk 30–40 mins daily + Stretching.

Mental Wellness: Meditation 10 mins daily.

Week 2 - Fertility Support

Focus: Reduce processed foods.

Add: Daily pomegranate and leafy greens.

Activity: Walk + Yoga 3x/week.

Week 3 - Strength & BP Control

Focus: Strength building and BP monitoring.

Activity: Strength Training 2x/week.

Mental Wellness: Fertility Journal.

Week 4 - Conception Preparation

Focus: High-protein meals and recovery.

Activity: Walk 45 mins + Yoga.

Mental Wellness: Gratitude Journal.
